# Rider–Waite–Smith card images

The 78 WebP files in this folder are the Pamela Colman Smith illustrations for the Rider–Waite tarot deck (also called Rider–Waite–Smith), first published in 1909 by William Rider & Son, London.

They were made from public-domain scans on Wikimedia Commons, category [Rider-Waite-Smith tarot deck (TaionWC)](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Rider-Waite-Smith_tarot_deck_(TaionWC)). Commons marks the files as public domain. Pamela Colman Smith died in 1951, and the 1909 publication is long out of copyright.

Each scan was scaled to 440 pixels wide, lightly denoised, and encoded as WebP (quality 52) so the static GitHub Pages site can show the deck on a phone without hotlinking to Wikimedia.
